Our Story

Empowering Farmers with Science-Backed Nutrition

Nature Power Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2024 with a clear mission — to give Indian poultry farmers access to premium quality, scientifically formulated feed supplements at the right price.

Based in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, we work directly with farmers to understand their challenges and build products that deliver real, visible results in bird health, growth, and productivity.
